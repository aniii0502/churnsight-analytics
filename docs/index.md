# ChurnSight Analytics

ChurnSight Analytics is a production-grade customer retention and churn analysis platform tailored for subscription-based SaaS businesses. 

While basic analytics tools often calculate a naive "churn rate" by dividing cancellations by total users, this approach fails to capture the nuances of user lifecycles, right-censored data, and cohort-specific trends. ChurnSight bridges this gap by applying rigorous statistical modeling to raw subscription data, providing actionable intelligence on customer longevity and behavioral segmentation.

This documentation covers the theoretical foundations of our survival models and the technical architecture that powers the containerized application.
